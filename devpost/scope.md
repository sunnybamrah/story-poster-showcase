# Story Poster: limited family showcase

A parent and child sit together when calm and read about ONE difficult moment the child went through that day. That moment becomes a chance to connect, teach and learn together about handling that situation and similar ones in the future. Familiar family drawings, one short rhyme, a wall poster and optional practice support that purpose. Bonding and learning are aims, not measured benefits.

## Core journey: three main steps for the first story

Sunny said: “only 2-3 steps for first story” and “don’t add guards unnecessarily.” Keep essential checks in the background and avoid extra approval pages.

1. Create an account with an existing email address and a chosen password, or sign in. Show the short trial notice. The initial showcase is limited to 50 family accounts.
2. Add only the family members needed for the first story and choose the comic look. A photo or appearance description creates the saved drawings. The rest of the family can be added later.
3. Tell the moment by voice or typing, choose who joins and create the kit. Keep any needed clarification inline on this same screen. The AI must first use the information already given. No forced follow-up when the moment is clear.

The finished kit is the result, not another setup form. Later stories reuse the account and family drawings: tell a moment, then read the kit. Reading and practice stay optional and calm.

Reuse a fitting saved topic first. If none fits, automatically find and check suitable sources for a new general guide. Do not force a nearby topic into a different learning goal. Families can return to saved kits and leave optional feedback. Private stories, names, pictures and memories never enter the shared topic library.
