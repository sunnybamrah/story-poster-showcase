# POSTER-CHECK: run this before any poster goes into a final PDF
# AND RUN THIS OVER THE PANEL STRIPS TOO. On 2026-09-14 the four insults were
# correctly deleted from a poster and simply reappeared one page later inside a
# comic panel, drawn on a phone screen, because this check only looked at page 1.
# Every word in EVERY drawn image gets listed, including words on phone screens,
# signs, books, whiteboards and anything else in the picture.

The poster is ONE drawn image, so the image tool writes every word on it, not you.
On the last kit it invented 34 words nobody approved: four insults, three signposts,
four map slogans and the key word printed backwards. Every serious poster fault we
have ever found is one of those invented words. So:

1. LIST every word you can see in the drawn poster. Include words on signs, banners,
   objects, screens, badges and in the background. Miss nothing.
2. SUBTRACT the card text you actually wrote.
3. READ ALOUD whatever is left over.
4. DELETE every left over word and redraw. No exceptions, even if it looks nice.
   Decorative slogans teach nothing and they push the poster over the 110 word cap.
5. NEVER let an unkind word appear anywhere in the artwork. No insults, no mean
   messages, no name calling, even as the thing the story is about. The poster hangs
   on her wall for weeks where family and friends read it.
6. CHECK the key word is spelled correctly and is not mirrored or reversed.
7. CHECK the key word has not been used by an earlier chapter for a different meaning.
   Look it up in FAMILY-FACTS before you choose it.
8. COUNT the words. Over 110 means the drawing added some. Go back to step 1.
